Install in 3 simple steps

DairyDesk is distributed as an Android APK. Follow these steps to install it on your phone.

1
Download the APK
Tap the Download button on this page. Your phone will download the DairyDesk.apk file.

3
Install & Open
Open the downloaded file, tap Install, then open DairyDesk and create your account with your email.
